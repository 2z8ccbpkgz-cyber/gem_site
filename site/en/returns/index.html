Returns and exchange

Rules for orders placed on the site or through a manager (distance purchase), and for purchases in the showroom.

Withdrawal within 14 days

For a distance purchase you may withdraw from the purchase without giving a reason within 14 days of the day you receive the piece.

How to return

Tell us your decision in any convenient way: on Instagram @gemstone.md or to the manager who took your order. Send the piece back within 14 days of that notice: in person to the showroom (bd. Dacia 24/2, office 505, Chișinău) or by post/courier. The piece must be unused, in its packaging and with its certificate if one was included. You may examine and try it on as you would in the showroom.

Costs and refund

The buyer pays the cost of sending the piece back: by post within Moldova it is about 100 MDL. The standard delivery cost you paid to receive it is refunded. We refund within 14 days of receiving your notice, using the same payment method you used. We may wait until we receive the piece or you show proof that you sent it.

What cannot be returned

Pieces made to your individual order or personalised (for example to your size, your choice of stones or with engraving) cannot be returned under the right of withdrawal for distance purchases.

If the piece has a defect

If the piece does not match its description or has a defect, write to us. We will replace it, repair it or refund you as provided by the consumer protection law of the Republic of Moldova.

Showroom purchases

A piece of proper quality can be exchanged under the consumer protection law of the Republic of Moldova (Law No. 105/2003): unused, in its packaging and with proof of purchase. Ask the manager for details.
